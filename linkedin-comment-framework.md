# The Comment Engine — LinkedIn Comment Framework

## Goal
Five quality comments daily. 5 comments x 5 days = 25 in-niche placements weekly.

## Timing
First minute the post is live. Hit notification bell on 10-15 target HMs and creators.

## Structure
3-5 sentences each, written for the audience not the author.

Treat your comment like a mini-post:
1. **Hook** — an opening line that makes people stop scrolling
2. **Point** — add a missing point, a counter-argument, or a specific example
3. **Close** — end by addressing the author so they reply

## Rules
- Written for the **audience**, not the author
- Add substance: a missing point, a counter, a specific example from your experience
- End by addressing the author to invite a reply (question, tag, or direct address)
- Keep it 3-5 sentences — tight and punchy
- No generic praise ("Great post!", "Love this!", "So true!")
